## What the sources say
- **KPT board:** Keep (good, want to continue), Problem (want to fix), Try (how to improve); everyone contributes, the team discusses, and the session ends by making the Try a concrete action. Hold it regularly, at milestones. (Money Forward payroll)
- **Ground rules:** never doubt that each member did their best at the time; look for causes but do not assign blame; speak with 「私は こう 思う」. The facilitator shares the purpose first and draws out members who speak little. (june29, Scrapbox)
- **Small worries are welcome** as Problems; a Try is "a first step we can take", not everything; speak frankly. (online-hunting-union, Scrapbox)
- **Anti-patterns:** Tries pile up or are too coarse to carry out (decide the first action and a date); Keeps are scarce (thanks count); criticism from a newcomer can hurt relationships, so how you say it matters; for an incident, keep a timeline to look back on. (Zenn)
- **Prevention** after an incident = find the root cause and change the procedure, shared with everyone. (Money Forward work-efficiency)

## How it became the two variants
- a: 村上 facilitates Keep → Problem → Try. Each branch looks back on a different part of the course (asking early about the save error / 復唱 on the incident call / the team; the 3-day delay / the one-person update). A vague Try (「がんばります」) gets 「具体的には、いつ、何を しますか」. 藤田 calls the one-person update 「仕組みの 問題」. Free talk: going to 喫茶さかもと together.
- b: 青木 asks what was hard for a newcomer. The learner uses 「正直に 言うと、〜」, gives an example, and proposes something for the team; a self-only answer (「もっと 勉強します」) is accepted and the team asks for one thing *they* can do. The team thanks the learner; the course ends with 「これからも よろしく お願いします」.
