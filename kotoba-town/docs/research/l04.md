# Lesson 4 research notes — なんじから なんじまで

## Grammar taught
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | いま ～じ ～ふんです | clock time; はん for half past; ごぜん／ごご go before the hour. |
| g2 | Vます／Vません | polite non-past: habits and future. |
| g3 | Vました／Vませんでした | polite past and negative past. |
| g4 | N(time)に V | に after clock times (and optionally days of the week); not after きのう, きょう, まいあさ, いま. |
| g5 | N1から N2まで | start and end; either half may be used alone, and です can follow directly (5じまでです). |
| g6 | N1と N2 | "and" between nouns only. |
| g7 | ～ね | confirming (5じまでですね) or sympathy (たいへんですね). |
| g8 | なんじ／なんぷん／なんようび／なんばん | question words for time, day of the week and numbers. |

The only verbs available are おきます, ねます, はたらきます, やすみます, べんきょうします, おわります. There is no いきます／きます (lesson 5), no object を with a verb (lesson 6) and no あります.

## Key vocabulary
いま, ～じ, ～ふん／ぷん, はん, ごぜん, ごご, あさ, ひる, ばん／よる, おととい, きのう, きょう, あした, あさって, けさ, こんばん, やすみ, ひるやすみ, まいあさ, まいばん, まいにち, げつ～にちようび, なんようび, ばんごう, なんばん, デパート, ぎんこう, ゆうびんきょく, としょかん, びじゅつかん, そちら, たいへんですね.

## Real-life situations it prepares for
- Phoning a museum, library, bank or shop to ask opening hours and closed days.
- Reading an opening-hours sign and asking at a counter; asking the time.
- Giving and confirming a phone number.
- Small talk about daily routine and about yesterday (when you get up, how long you worked, days off).

## Common learner mistakes
- Hour readings よじ, しちじ, くじ (not よんじ, ななじ, きゅうじ).
- ふん vs ぷん: いっぷん, さんぷん, よんぷん, ろっぷん, はっぷん, じゅっぷん; ごふん, にふん, ななふん, きゅうふん.
- ごご 8じ, not 8じ ごご.
- に after きのう／まいあさ／いま, or no に after a clock time.
- ませんました instead of ませんでした; non-past for something that happened yesterday.
- と used to join sentences or verbs (it joins nouns only).
- Reading a phone number without の between the groups.

## How the variants map to the grammar
Town facts shared by the variants: Midori Art Museum is open 9:30–17:00, until 20:00 on Saturdays and Sundays, closed on Mondays; its café is open 11:00–16:30; a ticket is 1,200 yen.

| variant | scene / partner | story | grammar targeted |
|---|---|---|---|
| l04a びじゅつかんは なんじまで | `office`, phone call, 上田 on `phone` | Friday evening call from your desk: opening hours (from–to, or only one half), closed day, confirm the weekend hours with ね, then the café's hours or phone number. | g5, g8, g7, g6 |
| l04b きょうは 5じまで | `museum`, 上田 at the counter | Thursday 16:40 with a dead phone: ask the time or today's closing time, ask about tomorrow or the weekend, ask about the café (it finished at 4:30, past tense) or the closed day, then take a ticket for today or come back another day. | g1, g8, g5, g7, g4 |
