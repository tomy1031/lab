# b12 「圧迫面接」 — research notes

Episode 12 of ITビジネス編 (Story C 「就職の 面接」). Two patterns at the fictional 株式会社 くろがねシステム:
b12a 否定と 深掘り (黒岩 alone) and b12b 答えにくい 質問 (黒岩 + 白石).

## Sources read

1. **マイナビ転職「圧迫面接とは？」** — https://tenshoku.mynavi.jp/knowhow/mensetsu/guide/33/
   - Definition: the interviewer deliberately asks intimidating questions / behaves negatively to watch the reaction; becoming rarer
     (compliance); whether it "is" a pressure interview is partly the candidate's perception.
   - Why: real motivation (本音), flexibility with unexpected questions, stress tolerance, problem-solving under provocation.
   - Tactics used in the script: repeated denial of answers, relentless 「それは なぜですか」「具体的な 事例は」「根拠は」, flat reactions,
     「どうせ すぐに 辞めるんでしょ？」 (→ b12b 「2、3年で 辞めるのでは ありませんか」).
     The page notes that repeated follow-ups are often just a wish for a clearer answer (→ 黒岩's 「具体的には。あなたは 何を しましたか。」).
   - Answering: keep a positive face, think of it as a role, **accept the criticism first and then explain**, prepare for なぜ,
     ask for a moment instead of going silent (「少々 お時間を ください」 → tipsEn), a private/inappropriate question may be declined politely.
   - Not to do: show irritation, go silent, argue back (→ the `argued` / `accused` branches get a cold pause and the next question).
   - Its list of insulting lines (学歴, 「話が 下手だよね」, shouting) was deliberately **not** used: 黒岩 doubts and digs but never insults.

2. **ネオキャリア「圧迫面接」** — https://www.neo-career.co.jp/careertrus/appakumensetu
   - Purposes: stress tolerance, quick thinking under repeated follow-ups, communicating with an unfriendly counterpart, readiness for
     difficult clients (→ 黒岩's reveal: 「お客さまの 前でも 落ち着いて 話せるかを、見たかったのです。」).
   - Questions/tactics: 「あなたが 当社に もたらす メリットは」 (→ b12b), "not your first choice / not suited to us", 5W1H drilling,
     rapid questions without waiting, ending with a curt reaction (→ 「…わかりました。次。」).
   - Answering: start with 「おっしゃる 通りです」 rather than arguing; breathe, re-read the negative question as a normal one
     ("why us?", "can you cope?") and answer that; steady voice. NG: getting emotional, crying, walking out.

3. **リクルートエージェント「面接の流れと入退室のマナー」** — https://www.r-agent.com/guide/article12652/
   - Order used for both patterns: knock → 「失礼いたします」 → beside the chair 「〇〇と 申します。本日は よろしく お願いいたします」 →
     「お掛けください」 → 「失礼いたします」 and sit, bag on the floor beside the chair → self-introduction → motivation / experience / self-PR →
     the candidate's questions (逆質問) → 「本日は ありがとう ございました」 → 「失礼いたします」.
   - Arrive 5–10 minutes early (tips).

4. **厚生労働省 公正採用選考特設サイト「採用選考時に配慮すべき事項」** — https://kouseisaiyou.mhlw.go.jp/consider.html
   - 14 items that can lead to hiring discrimination: (a) matters the applicant is not responsible for — 本籍・出生地, 家族（職業・続柄・
     健康・地位・学歴・収入・資産）, 住宅状況, 生活環境・家庭環境; (b) matters of personal freedom — 宗教, 支持政党, 人生観・生活信条,
     尊敬する 人物, 思想, 労働組合・社会運動, 購読新聞・愛読書; (c) methods — 身元調査, unnecessary health checks, forms with unrelated items.
   - Used for: 白石's one inappropriate question (family's work), the feedback line 「家族の 仕事は、本当は 面接で 聞いては いけない 質問です。」
     and the tip.

5. **大阪労働局「不適切な質問の例」** — https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/osaka-roudoukyoku/hourei_seido_tetsuzuki/shokugyou_shoukai/hourei_seido/kosei/futeki.html
   - Concrete inappropriate questions: 「あなたの 家族の 職業を 言って ください。」「本籍地は どこですか。」「家族は 何を 信仰して いますか。」 and
     why (judging by things the applicant cannot change; prejudice). Confirms the choice of the family-occupation question.

## From search summaries only (not opened — treat as unverified)

- Labour-bureau material (Aichi) says nationality should not be collected during selection, while **在留資格 may be confirmed orally**
  and the residence card checked after the offer. Used only for the tip "being asked about your work permission (在留資格) is normal".
- Labour-bureau notes that most inappropriate family questions come from small talk rather than malice — the reason 白石 asks it flatly
  from her sheet instead of with hostile intent.

## Design decisions drawn from the research

- **Answer shape taught** (grammar g3–g7): 受け止める (おっしゃる とおりです／ご指摘 ありがとう ございます／ご心配は わかります) → 言い直す with a
  reason or example (具体的には、私は 〜を 担当しました) → 前向きに (今は 〜ように して います／御社の お役に 立てると 考えて おります).
- **Inappropriate question**: no source gave a model reply for candidates beyond "you may decline politely", so the three model answers
  are our own, built on that advice: (1) one neutral sentence then steer back, (2) polite decline (お答えを 控えさせて ください), (3) a brief
  answer tied to the motivation. The accusing reply is a branch, met with a cold pause — not rewarded, not punished.
- **Branches** follow the NG list: calm + evidence → curt acceptance or a deeper question; apology / vagueness → he presses
  (「それでは 答えに なって いませんね」); arguing → silence and the next question.
- Both patterns end with the reveal (わざと 厳しく) and one line of feedback, as the spec requires, so the scene teaches rather than humiliates.
- Added profile fact (b12b only, optional): the family lives in Phnom Penh and the parents run a small shop — so nobody has to invent
  family facts on the spot. The other profile facts are those of BUSINESS_SPEC.
