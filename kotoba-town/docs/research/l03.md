# Lesson 3 research notes — どこですか・いくらですか

## Grammar taught
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | ここ／そこ／あそこ, こちら／そちら／あちら | here by speaker / there by listener / far from both. The こちら set is the polite form and also means direction. If speaker and listener share the same space, that shared space is ここ. |
| g2 | N1は N2(place)です | location of a thing, place or person with です (no verb yet): でんわは 2かいです. |
| g3 | どこ／どちら | "where"; どちら is politer. |
| g4 | N1の N2, どこの N | N1 = country or company that made N2: にほんの くるま, どこの ワインですか. |
| g5 | いくらですか, ～えん | prices, with hundreds / thousands / ten-thousands. |
| g6 | おくに／うち／かいしゃは どちらですか | どこ／どちら (never なん) asks someone's country, company, school or home; お～ is the polite prefix. |
| g7 | Nを ください | shopping/ordering chunk (also ～を みせて ください as a chunk). |
| g8 | ～かい／なんがい／ちか | floors. |

There are **no verbs** in lesson 3. Every NPC line ends in です／で ございます or is a set phrase.

## Key vocabulary
ここ・そこ・あそこ・どこ, こちら・そちら・あちら・どちら, きょうしつ, しょくどう, じむしょ, かいぎしつ, うけつけ, ロビー, へや, トイレ／おてあらい, かいだん, エレベーター, エスカレーター, くに, かいしゃ, うち, でんわ, くつ, ネクタイ, ワイン, うりば, ちか, ～かい／なんがい, ～えん, いくら, ひゃく・せん・まん, すみません, ～で ございます, みせて ください, じゃ, ～を ください.

## Real-life situations it prepares for
- Finding a counter, restroom, elevator or floor in a department store or office building.
- Being shown around a new workplace or school.
- Asking the price and origin of goods, then buying (これを ください).
- Light small talk: which country you are from, where your home or company is.

## Common learner mistakes
- そこ vs あそこ: あそこ is far from *both* people; そこ is by the listener.
- なんかい for なんがい; 3がい, and the sound changes いっかい／ろっかい／はっかい／じゅっかい.
- Number readings: さんびゃく, ろっぴゃく, はっぴゃく, さんぜん, はっせん; 10,000 is いちまん, not まん.
- Using なん (おくには なんですか) where どこ／どちら is needed.
- Answering どこの ワインですか with a place *where it is* instead of where it is from.
- Using the clerk's ～で ございます about yourself. Customers just say です.
