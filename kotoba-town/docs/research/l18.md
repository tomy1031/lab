# Lesson 18 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| — | dictionary form | group I: the い-row sound before ます → う-row (かきます→かく, のみます→のむ, かいます→かう); group II: ます→る (たべる, みる); group III: する, **くる** |
| g1 | N／V-dictionary ことが できます (ability) | N must be a skill or action noun (にほんご, ピアノ, りょうり, うんてん) |
| g2 | V-dictionary ことが できます (possibility) | what a place or situation allows: カードで はらう ことが できます |
| g3 | わたしの しゅみは N／V-dictionary ことです | こと turns the verb phrase into a noun |
| g4 | V-dictionary／Nの／period まえに、V2 | always dictionary form, whatever the tense of V2 (にほんへ くる まえに、べんきょうしました) |
| g5 | なかなか + negative | "not easily"; here with ことが できません |
| g6 | ぜひ | strengthens wishes and invitations (ぜひ きて ください／ぜひ いきたいです) |

Level line used for NPC speech: lessons 1–18. The dictionary form appears only inside the three patterns above;
no た-form, no plain-style sentence endings, no ～と おもいます, no ～に なります.

## Key vocabulary
できます, あらいます, ひきます, うたいます, あつめます, すてます, かえます, うんてんします, よやくします, けんがくします, ピアノ,
～メートル, こくさい～, げんきん, しゅみ, にっき, [お]いのり, かちょう, ぶちょう, しゃちょう, どうぶつ, うま, へえ,
それは おもしろいですね, なかなか, ぼくじょう, ほんとうですか, ぜひ.

## Real-life situations
- Small talk about hobbies with a café owner, colleagues, neighbours (ごしゅみは なんですか).
- Saying what you can contribute (cook, sing, write kanji) and asking what a place allows (reserve, pay by card).
- Routines and customs before something: before eating, before bed, before coming to Japan.

## Common learner mistakes
- ます-form kept before こと／まえに: ✗ひきます ことが できます, ✗ねます まえに.
- こと dropped: ✗ギターを ひくが できます; or が dropped: ✗ひく こと できます.
- Hobby sentence ending in the verb: ✗しゅみは えいがを みます／✗しゅみは えいがを みるです (→ みる ことです).
- Tense matching before まえに: ✗にほんへ きた まえに (→ くる まえに); の forgotten after a noun: ✗しょくじ まえに;
  の added after a period: ✗1ねんの まえに.
- なかなか with an affirmative verb (the "quite" meaning belongs to a later level).
- Group II verbs treated as group I (✗おく for おきる "get up", ✗かる for かりる); ✗きる for くる.
- できます with を: ✗にほんごを できます (→ にほんごが できます).
- As in lesson 17: learners whose first language does not inflect verbs (Khmer among them) tend to keep one memorised
  form, so the ます → dictionary conversion needs explicit, repeated practice. (Teaching observation; no Khmer-specific
  study on this point was found.)

## Variants → grammar
| variant | story | learner turns → grammar |
|---|---|---|
| l18a `cafe`, 坂本 + 藤田 | Rainy spring lunchtime: hobby talk over old jazz, then an invitation to Saturday's little concert where Fujita plays. | hobby g3 · can you play? g1 · accept with ぜひ / decline g6 · (if accepted) reserve? pay by card? g2 · before bed g4 |
| l18b `classroom`, 早川 + アナ + ミン | Autumn morning: planning the school's international party — who can do what, the poster's kanji, when to come. | what you can do g1 · kanji: can / なかなか g1 g5 · what time you can come g2 g4 · hobby g3 |
| l18c `home`, 森 あや + 森 しゅん | Summer evening before dinner at the neighbours': chopsticks, cooking, how you learned Japanese, hobbies. Free talk: customs before meals. | chopsticks g1 · cooking: can / なかなか g1 g5 · before coming to Japan (past tense main clause) g4 · hobby g3 |

まえに is modelled in all three shapes a beginner meets: V-dictionary (寝る まえに, 食べる まえに, 来る まえに) and
Nの (コンサートの まえに, パーティーの まえに). l18c n5 is the deliberate trap for ✗きた まえに.
