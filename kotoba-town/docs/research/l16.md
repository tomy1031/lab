# Lesson 16 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | V1て、V2て、V3ます | actions in the order they happen, same subject; tense and politeness only on the last verb |
| g2 | V1てから、V2ます | "after V1": V1 is finished first; often used for routines and instructions |
| g3 | いadj ～くて、～ | joining an い-adjective to the next word; いい → よくて |
| g4 | なadj／N で、～ | joining a な-adjective or a noun: しずかで、きれいです／ベトナムの ひとで、がくせいです |
| g5 | N1は N2が adj | topic + its feature: カンボジアは たべものが おいしいです／ミンさんは せが たかいです |
| g6 | どうやって V ますか | asking a route or a method; the answer is a chain of て-forms |
| g7 | どの N／どれ | which (of three or more): どの ボタンを おしますか |

Also in the lesson (used in NPC lines, not a separate id): まず…／それから; particles with the new verbs
(でんしゃ**に** のります, でんしゃ**を** おります, バス**に** のりかえます, おかね**を** いれます); ～ばんせん.

## Real-life situations
- Asking station staff how to get somewhere and how to work the ticket machine (or an ATM).
- Describing a friend you are waiting for, a park, a town.
- Talking about your day in order: morning routine, plans, "after I go home".
- Describing your home town and how to travel there.

## Common learner mistakes
From the sources:
- Only the last verb carries tense and politeness; learners put ました on every verb in the chain
  (✗ たべました、みました、いきました as one sentence) instead of たべて、みて、いきました.
- ～てから needs the て-form: たべるから／たべたから mean "because", not "after". Wrong て-forms of group 1 verbs
  (かえって, はいって, のって — not ✗ かえて, ✗ のて).
- ～てから attaches to verbs only.

From my own teaching knowledge, not from a source I could find (I found no page specifically about Khmer or
South-East Asian learners, so treat these as hypotheses to check with the class):
- Joining adjectives with と, because one word for "and" covers nouns and adjectives in Khmer, Vietnamese and English:
  ✗ やすいと おいしいです → やすくて、おいしいです.
- Mixing the two endings: ✗ やすいで, ✗ しずかくて, ✗ きれくて (きれい is a な-adjective), ✗ いくて for よくて.
- N1の N2は ～ everywhere instead of N1は N2が ～ (✗ ミンさんの せは たかいです is understandable but not how people say
  it); が replaced by は twice (✗ カンボジアは たべものは おいしいです) or dropped.
- Particles with the transport verbs: ✗ でんしゃを のります, ✗ でんしゃに おります, ✗ バスを のりかえます.
- のって／のんで, おりて／おいて, きて／きって: the small っ and ん in て-forms are hard to hear, so steps get mixed up.
- Joining a good and a bad point with ～くて (✗ やすくて、まずいです); the textbook pairs like with like and uses が for contrast.

## Level check
NPC lines use lessons 1–16 only: no ない-form, dictionary form, た-form, plain style, ～とき or clause modifiers
(「せが たかい ひと」-type phrases are avoided; only adjective + noun as in 「あかるい ひと」). Fixed chunks: おでかけですか and
いって いらっしゃい (lesson 11), よかったです (lesson 12). Words outside the textbook list, glossed in vocab or obvious
from the scene: おしろ, きっぷ, きかい, そばや, バイク.

## Layout assumptions for the scene builders
- `station`: the castle line leaves from `platform_sign_2` (2番線, get off at しろやま駅), the temple line from
  `platform_sign` (1番線, change to a bus at みどり駅); the fare on the `ticket_machine` is 320 yen for both; the `train`
