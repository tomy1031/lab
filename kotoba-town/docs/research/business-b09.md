## What the sources say
- **Order of an apology:** say what you apologise for and admit the fault → cause and how it happened → concrete prevention → a short closing. Apology words alone, without the cause, do not convey sincerity and can look like avoiding responsibility; excuses, blaming the other side and vague or false facts are NG; do not repeat the apology at length at the end. (biz.ne.jp)
- **Plain words.** Explanations to users avoid technical terms. (Money Forward) → 「設定の エラー」 gets 「つまり、どういう ことでしょうか」; the plain version is "the setting had an expiry date and it expired this morning".
- **After recovery, tell:** when it started and when recovery was confirmed, which function and users were affected, what users need to do, what impact remains, the confirmed cause, the handling, and concrete prevention with a clear condition for "done". (ai-agent-x)
- **Prevention is a change of procedure:** cross-checks by more than one person, logs, managed deadlines — found by asking "why" beyond the surface cause. (Money Forward) → 2人で 確認 / 期限を カレンダーで 管理.
- **Cushion phrases** (恐れ入りますが, お手数を おかけいたしますが, 差し支えなければ, よろしければ) soften requests and offers; overuse sounds like a manual. (HugKum)
- **Offer something:** complaint guides say not to end with an explanation only but to give a feasible solution or alternative. (LINE WORKS AiCall, アイミツ) → the list of orders (a), a draft of the notice (b).

## How it became the two variants
- a (wrong price file): 西村 is understanding. Branches: apology first or not (村上 adds it if missing), plain or vague cause, fix with or without time/check, concrete or vague prevention (「具体的には、何が 変わりますか」), offer or not.
- b (expired setting): 西村 had a hard morning with customer calls. She asks what to tell the ~30 customers; the learner suggests a notice and offers a draft by 3 p.m.
- The cause is always "our side's mistake" (こちらの ミス); no person is named.
