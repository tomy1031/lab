# Lesson 13 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | Nが ほしいです | wanting a thing (also time, a holiday, a friend); ほしい is an い-adjective → ほしくないです |
| g2 | Vます-stem＋たいです | wanting to do something; the object's を may become が; conjugates like an い-adjective → ～たくないです |
| g3 | N(place)へ Vます-stem／N に 行きます・来ます・帰ります | purpose of going: かいものに, およぎに, しょくじに, みに |
| g4 | なにか／どこか | something / somewhere; を and へ after them are often dropped; negative answers use なにも／どこも ～ません |
| g5 | ご注文は？ → ～を おねがいします／べつべつに おねがいします | the restaurant chunks of the lesson: ordering and splitting the bill |

Also in the lesson (used in NPC lines, not a separate id): のどが かわきました／おなかが すきました,
少々 お待ちください, ～で ございます (staff's polite です), そう しましょう.

## Key vocabulary
あそびます, およぎます, むかえます, つかれます, けっこんします, かいものします, しょくじします, さんぽします,
たいへん[な], ほしい, ひろい, せまい, プール, かわ, びじゅつ, つり, スキー, しゅうまつ, おしょうがつ, ～ごろ,
なにか, どこか, のどが かわきます, おなかが すきます, そう しましょう, ごちゅうもんは？, ていしょく, ぎゅうどん,
[しょうしょう] おまちください, ～で ございます, べつべつに.
Added for the scenes (not in the textbook list): おかいけい, やきとり, うみ, いろ, なつやすみ.

## Real-life situations
- Ordering at a restaurant or izakaya and paying separately.
- Talking about weekend and holiday plans: where you go and what for.
- Telling a shop clerk or a friend what you want to buy, and deciding whether to buy it.

## Common learner mistakes
- を or は instead of が with ほしい (✗ パソコンを ほしいです).
- A verb in front of ほしい (✗ のむ ほしいです) — actions need ～たい. Learners whose first language has one
  word for "want" (Khmer ចង់, Vietnamese muốn, English *want*) tend to pick one of the two and use it for both.
- Keeping ます before たい (✗ のみますたいです), or forgetting です.
- Conjugating like a verb instead of an い-adjective (✗ たべたいじゃ ありません → ○ たべたくないです).
- Using ほしい／～たい for a third person (✗ かれは すしが たべたいです) — out of scope here; the scenes only
  ask about the learner's own wishes.
- Asking a superior or a customer ～たいですか／ほしいですか. Staff in the scenes offer with ～は いかがですか;
  only friends (藤田, 青木, あや) ask なにが ～たいですか.
- を instead of に for the purpose (✗ かいものを いきます), or で for the destination.
- The last three points about first-language transfer are the author's teaching knowledge; no source specific to
  Cambodian learners was found.

Level check: no NPC line uses the て-form, ～て ください, ～て います or ～ましょうか. 少々 お待ちください and
～で ございます are lesson 13 chunks. お会計は ごいっしょですか is a shop phrase added as vocabulary.

## Layout assumptions for the scene builders
- `izakaya`: the learner sits (`"learner": "sit"` on n1); 中野 stands at the table (`staff`), 藤田 sits across (`npc1`).
  中野 points at `menu_strips`; the bill is settled looking towards `register`.
- `cafe`: 青木 sits across the learner (`npc1`); 坂本 speaks from behind the counter and points at `cake_case`.
- `depato`: あや stands next to the learner (`npc1`) and points at `umbrella_rack`; 西村 is behind the counter.
  The item the clerk offers is not named in the lines (こちら), so any umbrella/bag prop near her works.
