# Lesson 5 — research notes (どこへ いきますか)

## Grammar
| id | pattern | notes |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | N(place)へ いきます／きます／かえります | direction particle へ, read "e"; only with motion verbs |
| g2 | どこ[へ]も いきません(でした) | question word + も + **negative** = "nowhere"; どこも and どこへも both fine |
| g3 | N(vehicle)で いきます ／ あるいて | means with で; on foot = あるいて, **no で**; question: なんで |
| g4 | N(person)と V ／ ひとりで | company with と; alone = ひとりで, **no と**; question: だれと |
| g5 | いつ | "when"; no に after いつ or relative time words (きょねん, らいしゅう, あした) |
| g6 | ～がつ ～にち, なんがつ なんにち | dates; irregular days ついたち…とおか, じゅうよっか, はつか, にじゅうよっか; specific dates take に |
| g7 | S よ | tells the listener something they don't know; can sound pushy if overused |

## Real-life situations
- Asking station staff whether a train goes to your stop, which platform, when the next one is, and how to continue (bus / on foot).
- Small talk with someone you meet: when and with whom you came to Japan, how you got here today.
- Monday-morning office chat about the weekend; birthdays; holiday plans (going home, how, when).

## Common learner mistakes
- Reading へ as "he" instead of "e".
- ✗ あるいてで いきます (no で), ✗ ひとりと いきます (ひとりで).
- ✗ いつに, ✗ らいしゅうに, ✗ あしたに — relative time words take no に.
- ✗ どこも いきます — どこも needs a negative verb.
- Using で (means) where と (company) is needed and vice versa; using へ with non-motion verbs.
- Irregular day readings (ふつか, はつか …) and しちがつ／くがつ.
- Boarding an express that skips the station — a practical, not grammatical, mistake that makes a good scene.
