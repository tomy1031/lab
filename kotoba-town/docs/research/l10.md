## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | Nが あります／います | existence; あります = things, plants, places; います = people, animals |
| g2 | N1(place)に N2が あります／います | "what/who is there?" — question words なに／だれ |

## Real-life situations
- Asking shop staff whether they stock something and which shelf it is on.
- Finding things and people at home or in the office; describing a room.
- Asking a passer-by whether there is a post office / bank / ATM nearby and where it is.

## Common learner mistakes
- あります for people or animals, います for things (✗ ねこが あります).
- で or no particle for the place of existence: ✗ えきの まえで カフェが あります → ○ えきの まえに.
- Dropping の before the position word (✗ テーブル うえ), or wrong order (✗ うえの テーブル).
- あいだ without "AとBの".
- と where an open list is meant (や); forgetting that など is optional.
- Using です for location where the verb is expected, and mixing the two word orders (が for new information,
  は for the known thing you are looking for).

## Layout assumptions for the scene builders
The lines state these positions, so the 3D scenes should match:
- `konbini`: `atm` next to `copy_machine`; `toilet_sign` to the right of `drinks_fridge`; umbrellas near `entrance`;
  batteries on the bottom shelf of the `magazines` rack; `cat` sits **on a box outside the window**.
- `home`: `spoon` lies on the `table` next to the `cake`; `photo` is by the hosts; `garden` has a cherry tree and flowers.
- `station`: `bus_stop_sign` visible in the distance; the post office / bank / supermarket are off-scene
  ("in front of the station": bank – post office – supermarket, left to right).
