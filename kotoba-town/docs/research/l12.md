# Lesson 12 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | N／な-adj でした・じゃ ありませんでした | past of noun and な-adjective sentences |
| g2 | い-adj ～かったです・～くなかったです | drop い; いい → よかったです, よくなかったです |
| g3 | N1は N2より adj です | より marks the standard of comparison (the "less" side) |
| g4 | N1と N2と どちらが adj ですか／N の ほうが adj です | choosing between two; どちらも for "both" |
| g5 | N1[の 中]で 何／どこ／だれ／いつが いちばん adj ですか | the most … in a group of three or more; the question word takes が |

Also in the lesson (used in NPC lines, not a separate id): ～は どうでしたか, おかえりなさい, つかれました, でも, はじめて,
ずっと, and a number before the adjective to give the difference (3,000えん やすいです).

## Real-life situations
- Reporting how something was: a festival, a trip, a party, the weather, the food.
- Choosing between two things in a shop and asking the clerk which is cheaper.
- Small talk that compares home and Japan (which is hotter, which season, which food you like best).

## Common learner mistakes
- Treating い-adjectives like nouns: ✗ おいしいでした, ✗ たのしいじゃ ありませんでした, ✗ あつくないでした.
  And the reverse with な-adjectives: ✗ にぎやかかったです, ✗ きれかったです (きれい looks like an い-adjective).
- ✗ いかったです／いいでした for よかったです; ✗ いくなかったです for よくなかったです.
- Forgetting the past altogether when the time word already says "yesterday" (✗ きのうは あついです).
- より on the wrong item, so the sentence says the opposite; using より twice; starting the answer to a どちら question with
  ～は instead of ～の ほうが.
- どちら for three or more things, or どれ／なに mixed up; は after the question word (✗ なには いちばん → ○ なにが いちばん).
- For Cambodian and other South-East Asian learners: Khmer adjectives do not change for tense (time is shown by a time word
  or a particle) and comparison is made with a word placed after the adjective ("big more than"), so the weak spots are the
  adjective endings themselves and the Japanese order "B より A の ほうが". This is our own inference from the structure
  of Khmer; we found no published error study on Khmer speakers for this lesson.
