# b05 遅れを 報告する — research notes

Episode 5 of Story A. Skill: reporting a schedule delay early, in the order
結論 → 理由 → 対策 → 見通し, and bringing options when the release date is at risk.

## Sources read

- https://zenn.dev/gachigachi/articles/996839cdbed815 — reporting a delay as Fact → Cause → Action → Impact;
  what damages trust is a late report more than the delay itself; after reporting, carry out the recovery
  plan and keep reporting progress.
- https://dime.jp/genre/1820424/2 — progress reports: state the status; for a delay add the reason and what
  you want the other person to do; when asking to move a deadline name a concrete date; add how you will
  prevent it next time.
- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/shigoto/it/point9.html — Ministry of
  Health, Labour and Welfare guidance for IT projects: first make the extra work/cost visible and share it
  with the people concerned, then decide who bears it and whether to go ahead; the project manager reports
  to management and coordinates with the client.

Could not open (HTTP 403, not used as a source): atsoho.com/blog/freelance-935. Its search snippet said to
give the conclusion, the new date, the affected range and a recovery plan before the cause, because
starting with the cause sounds like an excuse — the same point the sources above make.

## What went into the scenario

- 結論から with a number (g1): if the learner only apologises, 村上 says 「まず 結論から 教えて ください。
  何が、どのくらい 遅れますか」; 「少し」 is called out as impossible to plan around.
- 理由 (g2) as a fact, not self-blame: when the learner blames themselves, 村上 answers
  「自分を 責めなくて いいですよ」 — the cause is the date-format issue, not a person.
- 対策 (g3): concrete action vs. 「がんばります」. 見通し (g4) always as a date (12月4日).
- Variant b: impact on the release (g5), two options (g6), recommendation with a reason (g7). 小川部長 reacts
  differently to each recommendation (keep the promise to the client / quality first / let the client
  choose), and asks for options if none are offered.
- Ending line: 「悪い 報告を 早く して くれて、ありがとう ございます」.
