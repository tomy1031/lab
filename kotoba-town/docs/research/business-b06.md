# b06 デモと 仕様変更 — research notes

Episode 6 of Story A. Skill: demoing to a non-technical client and handling a change request —
accept a small one with an estimate, take a big one back (期間・費用) without promising on the spot.

## Sources read

- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/shigoto/it/point7.html — Ministry of
  Health, Labour and Welfare guidance for IT projects: clients often change specifications mid-project;
  refusing everything fails the client, accepting everything inflates the work. Agree rules for changes,
  confirm the content of a change on both sides, re-estimate anything outside the agreed scope.
- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/shigoto/it/point9.html — steps for
  extra work: make the added effort and cost visible, share it, present a new estimate and let the client
  decide whether to go ahead.
- https://nativecamp.net/heync/question/153521 — usage Q&A: 「持ち帰って 検討する」 is what you say when a point
  cannot be answered in the meeting and you will reply later (the example continues with when the reply comes).

Search results seen but not opened (not used as sources): English sales-demo articles (know what the
audience wants to see; when you can't meet a request, give next steps and alternatives). The demo opening
(say what you will show, follow the customer's steps, plain words) is common practice rather than taken from
one page.

## What went into the scenario

- Demo opening (g1) and steps in the customer's order (g2); 坂本 says 「むずかしい 言葉は 苦手でね」 when the
  explanation is technical.
- 復唱 before answering (g3): saying 「できます」 at once makes 村上 step in with
  「先に 内容を 確認しましょう」.
- a: small change accepted with a number and its impact (g4): 半日, release date unchanged.
- b: online payment. Three reactions to the first request (confirm/ask why, promise, refuse); the reason
  (no-shows) only comes out when asked. Then 「この場では お答え できません。持ち帰って、期間と 費用を
  確認します」 (g5), a reply date (g6) and a closing summary (g7). Promising on the spot makes 村上 correct it
  politely in front of the client.

## Facts used / introduced

- Shared: app さかもと予約; a = メモ field, 半日; b = オンライン決済, needs an estimate of period and cost.
- Introduced: booking steps in the app (date → number of people → time → 「予約する」 button); 坂本's reason
  for payment = customers who book and don't come; reply promised 金曜日までに; the release goes ahead with
  the current content. No release date is spoken in b06 (it differs between b05a and b05b).
