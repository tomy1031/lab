# Lesson 9 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches
| id | pattern | note |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | Nが あります | "have": time, an appointment (やくそく), an errand (ようじ), tickets |
| g2 | Nが わかります | object of understanding takes が |
| g3 | Nが すきです／きらいです | な-adjectives, not verbs; object takes が |
| g4 | Nが じょうずです／へたです | skill; じょうず is for praising others |
| g5 | どんな N | "what kind of" — asks for one member of a category |
| g6 | よく／だいたい／たくさん／すこし／あまり／ぜんぜん | degree and amount; あまり・ぜんぜん need a negative predicate |
| g7 | S1から、S2 | reason; から sits on the reason; the reason may stand alone (…から。) |
| g8 | どうして | why; answered with ～から; どうしてですか reacts to what was just said |
| g9 | ちょっと……／また こんど おねがいします | indirect refusal of an invitation; reply ざんねんですね |

## Real-life situations
- Being invited out by a colleague or classmate and accepting / declining politely.
- Small talk about tastes: music, sports, food, drinks.
- Talking about ability: languages, kanji, cooking, singing.
- Explaining why you study Japanese or why you cannot come.

## Common learner mistakes
- を instead of が: ✗ おんがくを すきです → ○ おんがくが すきです (same for わかります, じょうず, へた).
- あまり／ぜんぜん with an affirmative: ✗ あまり わかります → ○ あまり わかりません.
- から on the wrong clause, or a どうして answer without から.
- Refusing too directly (いいえ、いきません). Japanese refusals trail off (ちょっと……), add a reason and often
  leave the door open (また こんど).
- Calling yourself じょうず — sounds boastful; the modest form is すきですが、あまり じょうずじゃ ありません.
