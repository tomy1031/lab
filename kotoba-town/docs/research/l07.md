# Lesson 7 — research notes

## What the lesson teaches

| id | pattern | notes |
|---|---|---|
| g1 | N(tool / means / language)で V | はしで 食べます, パソコンで 書きます, 日本語で 書きます. Question word: なんで. |
| g2 | 「word」は ～語で なんですか | Also これは 日本語で なんですか for an object you can't name. |
| g3 | N(person)に あげます／かします／おしえます／でんわを かけます | に marks the receiver. With でんわを かけます a place (会社に) is also possible. |
| g4 | N(person)に／から もらいます／かります／ならいます | に or から marks the source. For an organisation (bank, school, company) only から. |
| g5 | もう Vました ／ いいえ、まだです | "already" / "not yet". |
| g6 | Visiting set phrases | ごめんください, いらっしゃい, どうぞ おあがり ください, しつれいします, ～は いかがですか, いただきます, ～、すてきですね. |

Grammar still off-limits here: adjectives (L8) apart from the chunks すてきですね / いいですね, あります／います (L9–10),
すき (L9), から for reasons (L9), て-form (L14).

## Real-life situations that fit

- Dropping in on a neighbour with a souvenir from a trip (おみやげ) and being invited in for tea.
- Office talk the day after a birthday: who gave you what, what you give your family.
- Classroom logistics: lending a pencil, a borrowed dictionary, asking how to say a word.

## Home-visit etiquette

- Call ごめんください at the door; the host answers いらっしゃい and どうぞ おあがり ください.
- Answer しつれいします (おじゃまします is the everyday equivalent), take your shoes off in the genkan and
  turn them to face the door. Wear socks; put on the slippers if they are offered.
- Bring a small wrapped gift (てみやげ / おみやげ), typically sweets. Hand it over with これ、～です。どうぞ.
  The host may not open it in front of you.
- Accept the drink you are offered; say いただきます before eating or drinking.
- Wait to be shown where to sit.

## Common learner mistakes

- Answering もう ～ましたか with いいえ、～ませんでした. That means "I didn't (at all)"; "not yet" is いいえ、まだです.
- Saying あげます straight to the receiver (これを あげます). It sounds condescending; use どうぞ.
- Using あげます for something that comes to the speaker. At this level the safe pattern is
  (わたしは) ～に ～を もらいました. (くれます is lesson 24.)
- Particle direction: に marks the receiver with あげます／かします／おしえます but the giver with
  もらいます／かります／ならいます.
- Mixing up かします (lend) and かります (borrow).
- Using を or と instead of で for the tool (✗ はしを 食べます).
